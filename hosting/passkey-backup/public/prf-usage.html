This passkey can restore a Gleec Wallet

You created it in the Gleec Wallet app as a recovery passkey. It unlocks an encrypted copy of one wallet, so you can open that wallet again on a new device.

Before you delete it

If you delete this passkey, that encrypted copy can never be opened again. Your wallet itself is not deleted, and you can still restore it with its recovery phrase.

To keep a working backup, set up a new passkey backup in the app first: Settings → Security → Passkey backup.

Who can use it

  • You, on any device where this passkey is available.
  • Anyone you share this passkey with. Sharing it shares the wallet.
  • Anyone who gets into the account that syncs your passkeys, together with your device passcode or PIN.

If you think someone else has this passkey, move your funds to a new wallet.

This site only helps your devices recognise the Gleec Wallet app. It has no sign-in and stores nothing.
